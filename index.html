Common Questions

Aircon Cleaning FAQ — Makati 2026

Mga madalas na tanong tungkol sa aircon cleaning at repair sa Makati.

  • Tagalog: Magkano ang presyo ng aircon cleaning sa Makati?

    Aircon cleaning in Makati typically costs ₱1,200–₱2,500 per unit, depending on the type and size. Window units are cheaper (₱800–₱1,200), while split-type 1.5HP units run ₱1,500–₱2,000. Cassette or larger units can reach ₱3,000–₱4,000. Most shops charge an additional ₱300–₱500 service call fee. Package discounts of 10–15% are available when booking 2+ units.

  • Tagalog: Gaano kadalas dapat linisin ang aircon?

    Professional cleaning every 3–4 months for regular home use, or every 2 months if you use it heavily (8+ hours daily). Most Makati residents follow a quarterly schedule: January, April, July, October. During peak summer (March–May), every 2 months is recommended.

  • Tagalog: Kaya ko bang linisin ang aircon nang mag-isa?

    Partially yes. You can clean the front filter yourself every 1–2 weeks: remove it, rinse with water, air-dry, reinstall (10 minutes, costs nothing). However, deep cleaning of the evaporator coil, condenser, and compressor requires professional equipment and risks voiding your warranty if done incorrectly.

  • Tagalog: Bakit hindi na malamig ang aking aircon?

    Most common causes: (1) Clogged air filter — clean or replace it; (2) Low refrigerant — requires professional recharge (₱1,500–₱3,500); (3) Dirty evaporator coil — book a deep clean; (4) Blocked condenser (outdoor unit) — clear debris; (5) Broken compressor — usually requires unit replacement. Most issues cost ₱1,000–₱3,000 to fix.

  • Tagalog: Paano masigurado na lehitimo ang technician?

    Look for: (1) DTI registration — ask for their business permit; (2) Google/Facebook reviews — 4.5+ stars and 20+ reviews; (3) Written warranty — 1-month cooling guarantee in writing; (4) Uniform/ID badge; (5) Transparent pricing before work begins. Avoid cash-only-upfront technicians with no reviews.

  • Tagalog: Gaano katagal ang isang aircon cleaning session?

    Window AC: ~45 minutes. Split-type unit: 1–1.5 hours (indoor + outdoor). Cassette or larger units: 2+ hours. If refrigerant recharge is needed, add 30 minutes. Plan for 2–3 hours total to be safe, especially during peak season.

  • Tagalog: Mayroon bang warranty pagkatapos ng cleaning?

    Yes. Most legitimate aircon services include a 1-month cooling guarantee. If your aircon doesn't blow cold air within 30 days, the technician returns free of charge. Always ask for a written receipt. Extended warranties (3–6 months) are available for ₱500–₱1,000 extra.

  • Tagalog: Kailangan bang mag-book nang maaga?

    24-hour advance booking is recommended. During peak summer (March–May), book 2–3 days ahead. During off-season (June–December), same-day or next-day bookings are often available. Emergency same-day service costs ₱300–₱500 extra.
